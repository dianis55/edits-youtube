Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026.

edits-youtube is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels it signs in to.

What it accesses

With the permissions granted at sign-in, it reads the signed-in channel's videos and YouTube Analytics reports, and uploads, schedules and updates that channel's own videos, thumbnails and comments.

Where the data goes

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in the operating system's credential store. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

edits-youtube's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/dianis55/edits-youtube.
